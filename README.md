# Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior in the telecom industry using Power BI. The dashboard helps identify customer retention patterns, high-risk customer groups, and key factors contributing to customer churn.

The goal of this project was not just to create a dashboard, but to solve business problems related to customer retention and support data-driven decision-making through reporting and visual analytics.

---

# Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge for telecom companies because losing customers directly impacts revenue and increases customer acquisition costs.

The company faced difficulties in:
- Identifying customers likely to churn
- Monitoring retention performance
- Understanding customer behavior patterns
- Tracking churn trends across different contract types
- Analyzing factors affecting customer retention

This project was developed to analyze churn trends and provide actionable insights for improving customer retention strategies.

---

# Objective

The objective of this project was to:
- Analyze customer churn patterns
- Identify high-risk customer groups
- Monitor retention KPIs
- Understand the impact of contracts, tenure, and services on churn
- Support business decision-making through interactive reporting

# Dashboard Analysis

## Customer Retention Overview
The dashboard provides a clear retention summary showing retained vs churned customers.

## Churn by Contract Type
Month-to-month contract customers showed significantly higher churn rates compared to yearly contracts.

## Churn by Tenure Band
Customers with shorter tenure periods had the highest churn probability.

## Online Security & Backup Analysis
Customers without online security and backup services showed increased churn risk.

## Monthly Charges Analysis
Higher monthly charges were associated with higher churn rates.

---

# Key Insights Generated

## Insight 1
Month-to-month customers contributed the highest churn percentage.

## Insight 2
Customers with tenure between 0–6 months showed maximum churn risk.

## Insight 3
Customers without online security services were more likely to churn.

## Insight 4
Higher monthly charges increased the probability of customer churn.

## Insight 5
Long-term contract customers demonstrated stronger retention behavior.
